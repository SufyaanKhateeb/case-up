# Personal mark and logo exploration

The arrow/shooting star represents Sufyaan’s aspiration to rise in the world.
It remains the primary mark in `app/icon.svg`, the homepage identity, and the
footer’s back-to-top link. `components/portfolio/shooting-star.tsx` shares its
original path. Do not replace it with the sunglasses concepts without a decision
from Sufyaan.

Exploratory vector assets, rather than active identity replacements:

- `public/brand/sunglasses-concept.svg`: a single-color Wayfarer-inspired symbol.
- `public/brand/sunglasses-star-concept.svg`: the star as a lens reflection.
- `public/brand/logo-exploration.svg`: comparison at large and favicon sizes,
  plus a header mockup. The star reflection is a detail for larger uses; at 16px
  the simpler silhouette is more reliable.

Possible next uses of the star (suggestions, not implemented):

- Replace the generic diagonal arrows on project links with the signature.
- Use a small star and a fine trailing rule between major sections.
- Give the hero one brief upward star movement across its open grid space,
  with a stationary version under reduced motion.

Choose one or two accents. The character and scroll choreography should remain
the main source of motion.
