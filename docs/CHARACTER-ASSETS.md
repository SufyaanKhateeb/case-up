# Character configuration and maintenance

The subsequently supplied parted and Mixamo FBXs have isolated review routes at
`/character/parted` and `/character/rigged`. Their preparation and current limits
are documented in [the rigged model review](design/rigged-fbx/README.md). They do
not yet replace the active character described below.

The active website character is `public/character/arms-continuous.glb`, selected
by `content/character.ts` (version 7). The user approved moving the connected
left/right arm study into the website while retaining hand animation. This
supersedes the earlier temporary restriction that kept both arms rigid.

The asset retains the approved smoothed character's original continuous 200k
surface and textures with a seven-bone body/arm skin. `imported-model.ts` preserves
those sleeve weights and bind matrices, adds Neck/Head bones at runtime (nine
bones total), and blends head weights underneath the angled jawline. The field
`sourceY + .28 * sourceZ` transitions from body to head between .480 and .495;
this keeps the actual low/front chin rigidly on Head and leaves the collar fixed.
Do not restore a height-only transition: the chin reaches y≈.471, well below the
earlier check at .55. Geometry stays in source units under a registration group
(scale 7, source head centre .66 at controller height 4.55); no geometry baking
or procedural Euler transfer is required.

`connected-arm-motion.ts` adds explicit degree-based poses to the existing owned
GSAP timelines: waves, pointing/think/watch gestures, both-arm cheer, swipe,
dance, panic, flight, selfie and drag reactions. The tested limits remain
shoulder 0–35°, elbow 0–70° and wrist ±25°. Rest, reduced motion and interruption
use the same lifecycle. Head, root travel, badge clipping and sound still follow
the existing controller. Central chest/waist stay rigid within the mesh.

The procedural surfaces remain hidden. `facial-expressions.ts` generates six
relative position blendshapes for the visible mesh: blink, wide eyes, independent
brows, smile and an open-mouth reaction. These use measured local masks, retain
all UVs and base geometry, and read the existing face/mouth/blink channels.
The low chin, neck, nose, hair and clothing remain outside the expression masks.
This is a restrained facial pass, not full lip-sync or individual finger posing.
Complete hands still move through their wrists and arm chains.

Downward head pitch is removed from the watch cue, pointer tracking, idle and
trick cues. `safeHeadPitch` guards all three live head/neck inputs in [-.12, 0].
The hat is removed entirely. Charcoal oval headphones with cream trim rest around
the collar on Body; sunglasses follow Head and the watch follows LeftHand.
`lib/character-accessories.ts` is the shared UI/drop catalogue. One active model
and canvas are rendered. Owned imported geometry, maps, bitmaps and skeleton are
disposed with the scene, while accessory resources remain controller-owned.

`/character/articulation` remains a separate manual review of the same seven-bone
asset, with independent arm controls and a fixed head. See
[`design/connected-arms/README.md`](design/connected-arms/README.md) for preparation,
limits and validation. Dense reconstructed cloth still compresses at stronger
inner-elbow bends; no cuts or caps are introduced. Earlier source/export files
remain intact.

The matching transparent fallback is `public/character/smoothed-poster.png`.
It is shown while the GLB loads, on loading failure and without WebGL. Re-export
it from `/character/hunyuan?poster` using "Download fallback PNG". That mode
renders the approved model with a 5.75-unit square orthographic camera, head
centre at 50% X / 40% Y, 512px output and the same PBR lights/ACES exposure as
the live scene. The previous procedural poster remains intact.

The approved color concept supplied the original design direction.
Identity cues come from `public/msk_cartoon.png`; the concept is in
`docs/design/character-msk-concept.png`.
The reference uses procedural Three.js geometry and GSAP poses, not a GLB.
See `CHARACTER-REFERENCE.md` for source inspection and provenance.

An additional, isolated Hunyuan study is available at `/character/hunyuan`.
It compares a single-image 2.1 mesh with geometry generated by 2mv Turbo from
four AI-generated turnaround views. See `design/hunyuan/README.md` for inputs,
reproducible authoring scripts, generation records, limitations, and licensing.
The comparison now defaults to the user's smoothed higher-quality full model,
cleaned from `generated_full_high_quality.glb` using `clean-textured-model.py`
and exported as `public/character/sufyaan-full-hq-smooth.glb`. It uses 200,000 runtime
triangles, explicit tangents and 4K WebP PBR maps. The original 1.5-million-
triangle density and packed 4K textures remain in
`artifacts/character/full-model-hq/sufyaan-full-hq-smooth.blend`. Five gentle
alternating smoothing passes are applied before runtime decimation. The
unsmoothed `sufyaan-full-hq.glb` remains selectable. The previous `generated_full.glb` derivative remains
available as `public/character/sufyaan-full.glb` (50k triangles, 2K maps).
The earlier bust,
optimized from `generated_model.glb` using `prepare-supplied-model.py`, is still
selectable. All original supplied files remain untouched.
Its local Draco decoder is bundled with its license. The GLB itself has no
skeleton or animation clips; the live adapter supplies skinning at runtime.
The inspection page displays the unrigged asset. Earlier studies remain
available for comparison.

## Editable sources

- `content/character.ts`: hair, beard, palette, and fallback URL.
- `components/character/imported-model.ts`: approved mesh registration, spatial
  skin weights, bone/controller transfer, fitted accessory mounts and disposal.
- `components/character/model.ts`: geometry, spherical face anchors, dynamic
  mouth, chin beard, separated moustache, accessories, articulated hands.
- `components/character/quiff.ts`: a cropped cap and five swept, tapered locks.
- `components/character/sweatshirt.ts`: straight, relaxed torso, rounded shoulders,
  ribbed crew neck, and a shooting-star chest emblem conforming to the fabric.
  Matching sleeves/cuffs use the existing articulated arm pivots in `model.ts`.
- `components/character/sunglasses.ts`: thick Wayfarer-style frames, opaque
  smoked lenses, reflections, camera details, and temples. The bow tie is removed.
- `components/character/motion.ts`: facial/hand presets and owned timelines for
  tour gestures, grabbing, and all eleven tricks.
- `components/character/scene.tsx`: lights, camera, antialiased color framebuffer
  and a cropped 256px badge target. Both use four-sample MSAA and byte textures
  to bound memory. Output DPR is capped at 2; the main target at 1.5.
  Both targets are disposed.
- `components/character/composite.ts`: transparent color output, circular clipping,
  and vanish/photo trick effects, with a single linear-to-sRGB conversion.
- `lib/character-render.ts`: the camera/UV crop. The compact face keeps its
  own detailed render rather than magnifying pixels from the full viewport.
- `public/character/poster.png`: transparent still rendered from the actual rig.

Keep each action within the controller’s 4.2-second window, or update the runtime
completion timer. New actions must reset to the latest tour chapter. Repeated
punches restart the action; direct manipulation interrupts other performances.
All child timelines belong to one owned parent and must be killed on interruption.
Dispose geometries, materials, instancing buffers, and render targets on teardown.

## Likeness and poster

The approved GLB is rendered as shaded 3D geometry, not as a billboard. Its
position, scale, turn, lean and circle crop use the established tour coordinates.
Keep `importedRegistration` and the poster framing aligned when changing assets.
The source-inspired monochrome pass remains retired. ACES tone mapping and
linear-to-sRGB conversion occur once in the final composite; do not apply them
again to the intermediate render targets.

The legacy procedural implementation and `public/character/poster.png` remain
available for comparison and rollback. Its procedural facial controls are not
the visible face of the new GLB.

## Sound and failure modes

Effects are synthesized locally. Audio defaults to muted, with a saved preference;
playback still requires a browser-allowed gesture. No reference recordings load.
The matching PNG poster covers scene loading, unavailable WebGL, and context loss.
HTML content and navigation remain usable. Reduced motion retains a static pose
and text feedback, and hidden scenes pause their rendering and timelines.
